    Soft sensing for bioprocess monitoring

    CellSENSING investigates soft-sensing approaches for estimating key physiological and process variables in biotechnological processes by combining sensor data with data processing, mathematical modeling, and machine-learning methods.

    Why CellSENSING?

    Cell viability and physiological performance influence both process performance and product quality. These important biological variables are often determined by offline analytical methods, which introduce delays and provide measurements only at a relatively low frequency. Soft sensing uses continuously available sensor and process information to estimate such variables indirectly, supporting a faster response to process changes.

    Application areas

    Yeast & brewing

    CellSENSING investigates yeast management and brewing-related processes, including physiological yeast characteristics and metabolic or process variables. The research scope includes yeast viability, yeast vitality, and beer-maturation metabolites such as diacetyl.

    Explore the yeast data

    CHO cell cultivation

    CellSENSING also investigates mammalian CHO cell cultivation in biopharmaceutical production, including viable cell density and viability. These project goals extend beyond the particular variables shown in this data application.
